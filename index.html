Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Curio Dig: Fossils & Gems ("Curio Dig") is a single-player game made by TieChan Software. It has no accounts and no server of our own. We, the developer, do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Google AdMob, which shows the ads, does collect some data — see Advertising below.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (finds, museum layout, coins, stardust, an unfinished dig, language and sound settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive a copy. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Save code

Settings → Export save turns your game progress into a text code and copies it to your clipboard so you can move to another phone. The code contains only game progress, no personal data, and it leaves your phone only if you paste it somewhere yourself.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, an occasional full-screen ad between digs, and reward videos that play only when you tap them. To show ads, Google AdMob collects and shares:

  • An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name)
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

In-app purchases

Remove Ads and the decoration packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives from Google Play which items you own, so it can unlock them. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Secure data handling

Your progress stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Google AdMob and Google Play send their data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We do not run any server that stores your data.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no data about you. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone's settings deletes everything the app saved. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policy linked above; resetting your advertising ID breaks the link to past ad data.

Children

This game is rated for players 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
